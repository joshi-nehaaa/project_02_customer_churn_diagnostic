Customer Churn Diagnostic
Step 4: Insights, Interpretation & Recommendations

Objective:
	Translate churn analysis results into actionable business insights and practical retention strategies for a SaaS business, ensuring findings are decision-oriented rather than purely analytical.

Data Source:
	Cleaned customer churn dataset (churn_clean.xlsx) derived from public Telco churn data, interpreted as a SaaS context.
	Quantitative churn metrics and segment-level analysis from Step 3.
	Qualitative churn signals synthesized from observed patterns in contract type, tenure, pricing, and support usage.

Steps: 
	1. Insight Consolidation
		Combined insights from step 3 (Insight ID, Insight Type, Quantitative Evidence, Qualitative Signal). 
		Structured insights by theme (contract type, tenure, support, pricing).
	2. Business Interpretation
		Interpreted why specific customer segments exhibit higher churn.
		Focused on behavioral, structural, and value-perception drivers.
	3. Actionable Recommendations
		Developed targeted retention strategies aligned to each insight.
		Identified impacted stakeholders, estimated implementation effort, and assigned priorities.
	4. Decision-Oriented Synthesis
		Consolidated findings, implications, and recommendations into a single structured table to support management decision-making.
	5. Stated Limitations & Assumptions.

Key Findings: 
	Customers on month-to-month contracts exhibit significantly higher churn due to low switching costs.
	The highest churn occurs within the first 6 months, highlighting onboarding and early value realization gaps.
	Lack of technical support is associated with increased churn during service issues.
	Higher monthly charges correlate with churn when perceived value is unclear.

Outputs: 
	analysis/churn_clean.xlsx
		Sheet:insights_recommendation
			Key findings, Quantitative evidence, Qualitative signals, Business implications, Recommended actions, Impacted stakeholders, effort, and priority
